# Candidate implementation examples and templates

These are proposed optional aids. They are not requirements and will be included only after the committee approves their purpose and boundaries.

| Candidate | Intended use | Related traceability topics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| System inventory | Identify systems, interfaces, owners, data handled, and criticality. | 4.3; 5.4.4; 5.5. |
| Intended-use and risk assessment | Document why a system/change matters to test data and what evidence is proportionate. | 5.4.4.3; 5.5. |
| Validation/verification plan | Record scope, acceptance criteria, evidence, deviations, and approval. | 5.4.4.3.1-2. |
| Change-control record | Authorize, assess, test, implement, and retain evidence for a change. | 4.3; 5.4.4.3. |
| Access review | Review roles, approvals, revocation, and privileged access. | 4.1.4.3; 4.10; 5.4.4. |
| Backup and recovery test record | Record backup coverage, recovery test, result, and corrective action. | 4.10.1.4; 5.4.4. |
| Provider assessment prompts | Assess external LIMS/provider responsibilities relevant to AAVLD requirements. | 5.4.4.6. |
