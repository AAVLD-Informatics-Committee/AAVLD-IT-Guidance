# Proposed development governance

## Purpose

Define how the committee develops and approves the guidance. This process does not establish laboratory IT controls.

## Roles

| Role | Proposed responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Committee | Resolves substantive scope, policy, and technical questions; approves formal review and release stages. |
| Editor(s) | Maintains the canonical draft, incorporates resolved decisions, and manages editorial changes. |
| Contributors/reviewers | Raise issues, review pull requests and artifacts, and provide evidence or examples. |

## Working rules

- `main` holds the current integrated working text; a tagged artifact, not the branch head, is the reviewable draft.
- Issues record work, questions, decision requests, and dispositions. Pull requests record proposed text changes.
- Editorial changes may be merged after review. Substantive changes require a linked issue and recorded committee resolution.
- The canonical source should be text-based. Word/PDF are generated or otherwise identified release artifacts.

## Stages

1. Working Draft - editor-controlled; open issues may remain.
2. Committee Review Draft - tagged, stable artifact with an issue/decision summary.
3. Approved Guidance - required AAVLD approval recorded; version and changelog published.

## Decisions required

- Who may declare a committee decision and approve the final release?
- What approval mechanism and quorum apply?
- Is a stakeholder/public review required before approval?
