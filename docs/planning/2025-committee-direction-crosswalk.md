# 2025 committee direction crosswalk

This working crosswalk records how the 2025 IT Committee topics and motions are
represented in the update work. It is a planning aid, not a record of completed
committee approval.

| 2025 topic or motion | Current capture | Tracking | Status / next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Review rule

No row is complete merely because a draft section exists. A topic is complete only
when its associated decision, traceability, and final committee disposition are
recorded.
