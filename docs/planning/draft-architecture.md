# Draft architecture and conventions - proposed

## Proposed outline

1. Purpose, scope, audience, and use of this guidance
2. Relationship to AAVLD accreditation requirements
3. System inventory, roles, and risk-based prioritization
4. Data lifecycle: availability, confidentiality, integrity, and retrievability
5. Access, security, auditability, and operational environment
6. Software, LIMS, interfaces, and external providers
7. Validation, verification, change control, and records of evidence
8. Backup, recovery, continuity, and nonconforming work
9. Implementation examples and templates
10. Traceability and references

## Conventions

- Use `AAVLD requirement` only for a traceable restatement of the authoritative AAVLD source.
- Use `guidance recommendation` for committee-approved advice that assists implementation.
- Use `example` for non-mandatory patterns, forms, or scenarios.
- Do not make vendors, products, or an external technical framework mandatory by example or citation.
- Put broad technical detail in an informative annex when it would distract from the minimum accreditation-focused core.

## Decision points

- Confirm the outline and whether sections 3 and 5 should be combined.
- Approve the terms used for the three statement types above.
