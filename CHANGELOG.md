### 2026-10-02 - Working Draft 0.3

Incorporated subcommittee input recorded on project issues into `guidance/working-draft.md`:

- The guidance is not LIMS-specific and laboratories determine where it applies; added the inclusion test and a minimum list of commonly in-scope systems, including analyzer firmware (#16).
- Added a risk-based approach to validation and change control, with a severity/likelihood risk matrix and examples (#15).
- Added informative references to ISO/IEC 17025 as a cross-check (#18) and to reporting and data-exchange standards for interoperability (#17).
- Unresolved items remain marked as committee review pending.
